ProofPay
AI-verified milestone payments for freelancers
PayPal Sandbox

Clients fear paying upfront for work that never matches the brief. Freelancers fear delivering work and never getting paid. ProofPay fixes both: the client funds each milestone with PayPal, an AI verifies the deliverable against the brief's acceptance criteria, and only a passing verdict releases the money — as a real PayPal payout.

👤 Client funds with PayPal
🔒 ProofPay Vault $0.00 held safely
🤖 AI Gate judges the deliverable
💼 Freelancer $0.00 paid on proof

Money only crosses the AI Gate when the work proves itself.

1 Project setup & AI brief parsing

Describe the job in plain English. The AI Brief Parser splits it into priced milestones, each with explicit acceptance criteria the deliverable will later be judged against.

Click a milestone card to work on it in steps 2–5. Amounts are editable.

2 Fund the milestone with PayPal

The client pays the milestone amount through PayPal Sandbox Checkout (server-side order create + capture). Captured funds are held by ProofPay until the AI verdict releases them.

Parse a brief first, then choose a milestone to fund.
PayPal Sandbox is not configured. No payment can be taken yet. Set the PAYPAL_CLIENT_ID and PAYPAL_CLIENT_SECRET environment variables (see README → “Environment setup”) and reload this page. ProofPay never simulates or fakes a payment.

3 Freelancer submits the deliverable

The freelancer describes exactly what was delivered and links the work. Submitting runs the AI Verification Engine against the active milestone's criteria.

4 AI verification report

Every criterion is scored on evidence found in the deliverable. Measurable requirements (counts, word counts, file formats) are checked exactly, and failing criteria name what is missing.

No verification yet — submit a deliverable in step 3.

5 Release the payment

A passing AI verdict unlocks release: one click sends a real PayPal Payout to the freelancer's PayPal email. If the verdict is REVISE, the client can still override — but the AI's objections stay on the record.
